# Pedals Customer Analysis

## Project Overview
Analyzed customer purchasing patterns for a bicycle retailer to identify characteristics associated with higher bike purchase rates and potential customer-targeting opportunities.

## Tools
- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning and standardization
- PivotTables and purchase-rate analysis
- Data visualization and interactive dashboards

## Dataset
The project began with 1,026 customer records. After identifying and removing 26 duplicate records, the analysis used 1,000 unique customer records.

## Key Findings
- Overall bike purchase rate: 48.1%.
- Customers in the Pacific region had a purchase rate of 58.9%.
- Customers with a 2–5 mile commute had a purchase rate of 58.6%.
- Car ownership, region, commute distance, and number of children showed the strongest individual associations with purchasing.

## Recommendations
The findings provide a starting point for customer segmentation and targeted marketing. The observed associations do not establish causation and should be validated through further analysis and campaign testing.

## Project Status
Analysis and presentation completed as a training project. No measured business impact is claimed.
